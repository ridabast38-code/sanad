1) PANIC PATH — ENRICHED FINAL VERSION

🫶 Step 1: SAFETY ANCHOR
“Right now, focus on where you are.”“You are not in immediate danger in this moment.”

🧠 Step 2: THREAT REALITY CORRECTION
“Your body may still be reacting as if danger is happening right now.”“But this moment is different, and the danger is not present anymore.”“Let’s help your system settle down together.”

🧍 Step 3: FULL BODY RESET (ENRICHED — replaces “simple physical reset”)
👉 goal: reduce physiological arousal + regain body control
Guide the user step-by-step:
Press both feet firmly into the ground for 5 seconds 
Release your jaw (let teeth separate slightly) 
Drop your shoulders down slowly 
Place one hand on your chest or stomach 
Notice the natural movement of your breath without changing it 
If possible, gently stretch your fingers or open/close your hands once 
✔ key effect:brings attention from “threat body” → “controlled body”

👁 Step 4: STABILIZING ATTENTION LOCK (ENRICHED — replaces simple fixation)
👉 goal: interrupt panic loop + stabilize attention
Guide:
Look at one object in front of you 
Slowly notice 3 details: 
its shape 
its color 
its texture or surface 
Keep your eyes on it for a few moments 
If your mind drifts, gently return to the object 
Remind yourself silently: “I am here, right now.” 

🔚 End:
“Your body is beginning to settle back into safety.”
B) SHAKEN / FROZEN PATH — ENRICHED FINAL VERSION

🫶 Step 1: VALIDATION
“After something sudden, the body can feel frozen or disconnected.”

🧍 Step 2: BODY RESTART (ENRICHED — graded reactivation)
👉 goal: gently re-engage the nervous system without overwhelm
Guide slowly:
Start by moving just one part of your body (fingers or toes) 
Then gently shift your position slightly (no rush, small movement is enough) 
Roll your shoulders once or gently tilt your head 
Press your feet into the ground for a moment, then release 
Notice: “I can still move, even a little” 

🌍 Step 3: STABLE WORLD RE-ENTRY (ENRICHED)
👉 goal: rebuild connection to external reality gradually
Guide:
Look around slowly, without searching for anything specific 
Notice one thing that feels stable (an object, wall, surface, light) 
Let your eyes rest on it without effort 
Then notice one additional detail about it (color, shape, position) 
Remind yourself: “The world is still here, and I am here in it.” 
🔚 End:
“You’re gently reconnecting with yourself.”
C) THINKING TOO MUCH PATH — ENRICHED FINAL VERSION

🫶 Step 1: NORMALIZATION
“Your mind may be replaying or trying to understand what happened.”

🧠 Step 2: THOUGHT SEPARATION
“This is a thought, not something happening right now.”“You don’t need to follow it.”

📦 Step 3: MENTAL RELEASE (ENRICHED — structured disengagement)
👉 goal: actively interrupt the replay loop (not just “let it pass”)
Guide:
Imagine the thought moving further away from you 
You are not pushing it away — just not holding it 
If it returns, gently label it again as “just a thought” 
Allow your attention to loosen around it, not fight it 
✔ key effect:
reduces cognitive fusion 
weakens repetition loop 
avoids suppression (important) 

👁 Step 4: ATTENTION REFOCUS ANCHOR (ENRICHED)
👉 goal: replace mental loop with structured present focus
Guide:
Choose one object in your environment 
Look at it slowly for a few seconds 
Notice 3 simple details: 
shape 
color 
texture or surface 
If your mind pulls back, gently return to the object 
Say silently: “Right now, I am here.” 
✔ key effect:
breaks rumination cycle 
stabilizes attention network 
restores present-moment control 

🔚 End:
“Not every thought needs your attention.”
4) OTHER / NOT SURE PATH — FINAL REFINED VERSION

🫶 Validation
“That’s okay — you don’t need to explain how you feel.”

🧭 Step 1: LOW-PRESSURE STABILIZATION
Pause for a moment 
Let your body be as it is right now 
You don’t need to fix, analyze, or change anything 

🌫 Step 2: MIXED-STATE NORMALIZATION (IMPORTANT UPGRADE)
It’s okay if things feel unclear, heavy, or slightly distant 
You might not be able to name what you feel — and that’s okay 
Sometimes after intense experiences, feelings don’t come in a clear way 

🫶 Step 3: SIMPLIFIED PRESENCE SHIFT
 Notice that you are here in this moment, even if things feel unclear 
 You don’t need to understand what is happening internally right now 
 Let your attention settle gently, without trying to guide it 
 If thoughts or feelings appear, you don’t need to respond to them 
 Just allow this moment to exist without pressure
👁 Step 4: LIGHT ORIENTATION (non-intrusive)
 Slowly become aware of the space around you 
 Notice one neutral detail in your environment (light, color, shape, object) 
 Let your eyes rest there without effort or focus pressure 
 You don’t need to analyze it — just let it exist in your awareness 
 If your attention moves away, that is completely fine
🔚 End message
“You don’t need clarity right now — just being here is enough.”
